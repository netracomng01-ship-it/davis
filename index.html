ABOUT NETRA

Built to bring people, ideas and business together.

NETRA is a social-commerce platform designed to make it easier for people to connect, discover content, build communities and shop from businesses they trust — all in one place.

We are building NETRA around a simple idea: social interaction and online commerce should feel natural, useful and connected.

For people Connect with friends, communities and creators.
For businesses Showcase products and build lasting customer relationships.
For creators Share ideas, build an audience and create opportunities.
